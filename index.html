    About this tool

    QR Scanner & Generator is a free, browser-based utility from RDJ Publishers that lets you read and create QR codes in seconds. Use your device camera to scan a live code, upload an image to extract a hidden link, or generate a new code for any URL, plain text, phone number, email, or Wi-Fi credentials.

    The scanner works straight from the bottom navigation bar — point your camera at any QR code and the decoded content appears instantly, with one-tap access for URLs. The generator accepts any string up to roughly 7,000 characters and produces a high-error-correction PNG you can download and share.

    Your privacy comes first. Every scan and every QR code is processed entirely inside your browser. No images, no decoded text, and no generated codes are ever uploaded to our servers. We do not log the contents of your scans, we do not store camera frames, and we do not require an account.

    The tool is completely free to use, requires no sign-up, and runs on both mobile and desktop browsers. Switch to the dark theme, pick a front or back camera, and tweak sound or vibration feedback from the Settings tab.

    QR Scanner & Generator is built and maintained by RDJ Publishers, a small independent studio that publishes a growing collection of practical, no-nonsense web tools. Questions, bug reports, or partnership ideas? Write to us at rdjpublishers@gmail.com.

    How to use
    1. Tap Scan in the bottom bar and grant camera access to read QR codes live. Hold steady until the result appears.
    2. Tap Image to upload a photo, screenshot, or saved picture containing a QR code — we'll extract the content instantly.
    3. Tap Create to generate a QR code from any text or URL, then download it as a PNG ready to print or share.
    4. Tap History to revisit every code you've scanned or generated. History is stored only on this device and can be cleared any time from Settings.
    Frequently Asked Questions
    Do I need to install an app?

    No. QR Scanner & Generator runs entirely in your web browser. There is nothing to download, no extension to add, and no account to create — just open the page and start scanning.

    Is any of my data uploaded to a server?

    No. All scanning and generation happens locally on your device. Your camera frames, decoded text, generated codes, and history never leave your browser. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

    Does it work offline?

    The first visit needs an internet connection to load the page and the scanning/generation libraries. After that, the core scanning and QR generation features continue to work without a network connection.

    What kinds of data can I turn into a QR code?

    Any plain string up to about 7,000 characters — URLs, plain text, phone numbers, email addresses, Wi-Fi credentials in the standard WIFI: format, vCard contact cards, and more. Decoded URLs also become tappable links in the result panel.
